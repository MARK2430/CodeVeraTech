ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

Opportunities should not be limited by geographical boundaries.

Across the world, individuals, technology professionals, NGOs and foundations are willing to contribute to education and community development.

However, connecting those willing to help with institutions that genuinely need support can be difficult.

CodeVeraTech aims to bridge that gap by providing local project coordination, needs assessment, technology implementation and transparent reporting.

WHAT WE WANT TO ACHIEVE

Creating opportunities through technology.

OUR APPROACH

From partnership to practical impact.

Every proposed programme should follow a clear and accountable process.

01

Connect

Receive an enquiry from a potential partner or educational institution.

02

Assess

Understand the needs, resources and feasibility of the proposed programme.

03

Plan

Prepare a programme proposal, budget, responsibilities and implementation plan.

04

Implement

Deliver approved activities and monitor their progress.

05

Report

Share documented results, expenditure information and lessons learned.
